  <h2 align="center">About Me</h2>

  I design and build modern web experiences, from interface to interaction. Frontend is where I spend most of my time — React, Next.js, and the details that
  make a product feel finished: layout, motion, and type.

  I’m a Biomedical Engineering student at NIT Raipur. Alongside the degree I work in UI/UX and visual design, using Figma and Photoshop to shape an idea
  before it becomes an application. The work I care about sits between engineering and design — technology with a clear, human point of view.
